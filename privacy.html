Privacy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Your data stays private and never leaves your device.

The accessibility service

FeedBlock uses Android's accessibility service to block the feeds and short videos you have chosen.

It recognizes the open pageFrom the buttons and tabs shown by supported apps, and the address of pages opened in browsers.
It lets you block the pages of the apps you have chosenA screen covers them, and the sound is muted while a video is blocked.
It taps some buttons for youOnly to navigate, for example switching tabs, going back or closing a short video.
Your data stays private and never leaves your phoneNo message, post, search or password is recorded or sent: FeedBlock has no internet access.

What is stored

On the phone only: your settings, plus the time spent in and the number of launches of each supported app (36 days at most), for Home and Statistics.

This data is deleted when you uninstall FeedBlock.

What is shared

Nothing. FeedBlock has no account, no ads and no analytics, and passes no data to anyone.

If you write to me or report a problem, it is your email app that sends the message, and only if you decide to. I use this message only to reply to you and fix the problem.

Pro subscription

Payment is handled entirely by Google Play. FeedBlock never sees your payment information.

Contact

For any question about your data: support@feedblock.app
