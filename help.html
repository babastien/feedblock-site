Frequently asked questions

What does FeedBlock block?

The feeds that make you scroll endlessly: the Home feed and short videos (Reels, Shorts, Spotlight, the TikTok feed). Messages, profiles, search, notifications and posting stay available. You choose the apps and pages in Protection.

Can I watch a short video someone sends me?

Yes. You see the video you open (message, profile, notification); moving on to the next one closes the player, so you can't keep scrolling.

Why is the sound muted?

When a page with videos is blocked, the sound is muted so they don't play behind the block screen. It comes back as soon as you leave the page.

What's the difference between Free and Pro?

Free is free, with no time limit. It protects one app of your choice, with limited feed access (1 hour a day) and statistics. Pro protects all apps and adds websites in browsers, adjustable feed access, protected hours, the lock, positive actions and color halos. You can try Pro for free for 7 days.

Are my messages read?

No. FeedBlock detects tabs and buttons, not content. No message, post, search or password is recorded, and nothing is sent.

Does FeedBlock use a lot of battery?

FeedBlock runs in the background and uses little battery.

Is FeedBlock available on iPhone?

No. To block specific pages, FeedBlock needs to recognize what's displayed in other apps and show a blocking screen over them. Android allows this through its accessibility service and display-over-other-apps permission. iOS doesn't allow third-party apps to detect and block specific pages within other apps the way FeedBlock does on Android. FeedBlock is therefore only available on Android.

FeedBlock stopped working?

Your phone may have stopped FeedBlock to save battery, or because it ran into an error. Open FeedBlock, then Help > FeedBlock stopped working?: the app checks each point and tells you what to do.
