These terms apply to the FeedBlock app for Android, published by Bastien Ballestero EI (see the legal notice). By installing FeedBlock, you accept them.

1. The service

FeedBlock blocks the feeds and short videos of the apps and websites you choose, to help you scroll less. It uses Android's accessibility service, which you turn on yourself and can turn off at any time.

FeedBlock comes in two versions:

FreeFree forever. One protected app, with limited feed access (1 hour a day) and statistics.
ProAll apps, websites in browsers, adjustable feed access, protected hours, the lock, positive actions and color halos.

2. Pro subscription

Pro is a subscription of €2.99 a month or €23.99 a year, taxes included. The price shown in Google Play at the time of purchase applies.

Free trial. You can try Pro for free for 7 days. If you don't cancel before the end of the trial, the subscription starts and the first payment is charged.

Renewal. The subscription renews automatically at the end of each period, at the same price, until you cancel it.

Cancellation. You can cancel at any time in Google Play (Profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions). Pro stays active until the end of the period already paid, then FeedBlock goes back to Free.

Payment. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play, under its terms. FeedBlock never sees your payment information.

Pro gives immediate access to digital content. By subscribing, you ask for this service to start right away, and you waive your 14-day right of withdrawal, in accordance with article L221-28 of the French Consumer Code. The free trial lets you try Pro before any payment.
